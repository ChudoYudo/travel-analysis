Calculated from raw coordinates in your Google Timeline export. Each point's country is resolved with an offline point-in-polygon lookup against country borders (Natural Earth, 50m resolution), fetched once from cdn.jsdelivr.net the first time you generate a report — your export file with personal location data is never uploaded anywhere and is processed only in this page. Trips to the same country separated by less than 24 hours are merged into one (a common cause of such a gap is GPS/navigation lag near a border). This does not account for the exact legal interpretation of any residency-counting rule (continuous vs. cumulative time, whole vs. partial days) — treat it as rough draft data, not a finished legal calculation.
